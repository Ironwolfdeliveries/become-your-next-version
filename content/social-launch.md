## Pinned launch sequence

### 1 — What BYNV is

**Hook:** You do not need a new personality. You need a clearer system.

BYNV connects honest reflection, deliberate direction, repeatable action, and evidence of progress. Start with the free six-question Version Snapshot. Continue with a secure account, the deeper Architect Assessment, your Blueprint, Daily Focus, Challenges, and The Architects community.

**CTA:** Take your Version Snapshot at becomeyournextversion.com

### 2 — Version Snapshot versus Architect Assessment

The Version Snapshot is a six-question opening signal. It gives you a preliminary Version Score and a useful first focus.

The Architect Assessment is the deeper member baseline: 42 questions across seven domains. It drives your full Version Score and Architect Blueprint.

One helps you start. The other helps you build.

**CTA:** Start free. No account required for the Snapshot. Launch Access begins with 30 free days; the published membership sequence applies only after account creation and secure billing activation.

### 3 — The Architect identity

An Architect does not pretend to control every outcome. An Architect chooses what to build, creates conditions that support it, and reviews the evidence honestly.

The Architects community is for mutual growth, useful accountability, and meaningful progress—not performance or artificial status.

**CTA:** Begin Launch Access and enter the Community. The first 30 days are free, days 31–90 are $19.99/month, and Foundation is $29.99/month from day 91 once billing is activated.

## First ten posts

1. **Seven domains, one life:** introduce Clarity, Energy, Action, Resilience, Relationships, Environment, and Reflection.
2. **Kai’s rule for assessment:** Kai can explain a question, but will never choose an answer for you.
3. **Daily Focus:** one priority, one realistic action, one end-of-day reflection.
4. **Version Score:** a current self-reported signal, not a diagnosis or permanent label.
5. **Minimum viable momentum:** make the next action small enough to repeat.
6. **Community with standards:** share questions, commitments, lessons, and milestones while protecting private data.
7. **Challenge check-in:** ask members what they repeated—not how many points they earned.
8. **Blueprint:** your strongest foundations, priority areas, and first actions from the deeper assessment.
9. **First Circle:** founding members help shape early BYNV through useful participation, not an empty badge.
10. **Architect Cycle:** choose a focus, define a period, act, review, and begin the next iteration wiser.

## Recurring formats

- **Kai clarifies:** one BYNV concept explained in plain language.
- **Architect check-in:** a single useful reflection question.
- **Evidence of progress:** practical milestones that are not follower counts or streak theater.
- **Inside the system:** short demonstrations of Snapshot, Blueprint, Daily Focus, Challenges, Progress, and Community.
- **Member voice:** only with explicit written permission; never reveal scores, assessment answers, journals, Blueprints, or Kai conversations by default.
